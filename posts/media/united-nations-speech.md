## United Nations Speech

Speaking at the United Nations on ‘AI and the Future of MSMEs’ was a profound honor, spotlighting how AI can transform micro, small, and medium enterprises—vital engines of global economies. ([Viewable on UN TV](https://webtv.un.org/en/asset/k17/k17v91jhnv?kalturaStartTime=7775).) With technology advancing relentlessly, AI offers MSMEs tools to streamline operations, uncover insights, and drive innovation, unlocking growth in ways previously out of reach. My talk explored how these businesses can harness AI’s potential, from predictive analytics to automated processes, to compete and thrive in a rapidly evolving landscape, ensuring resilience and opportunity without losing their human core.
